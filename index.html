How these scores are calculated

Every activity starts at 100 points. Some conditions are hard fails — they mark the day red immediately, no matter what else is going on. Everything else is a smaller deduction from the starting score. The final score sets the colour: 70 or above is green (good), 40–69 is amber (ok), and anything lower — including any hard fail — is red (bad).

Swim: hard fails are darkness, thunderstorms, more than 10mm of rain in the last 48 hours, waves over 1.5m, or a sea temperature below 4°C. Softer deductions apply for waves between 1–1.5m, waves between 0.6–1m, a sea temperature of 10°C or below (shown as a cold-water warning), rain 3–10mm in the last 48 hours, strong wind, low tide, rain currently falling, and cool air. Wind direction and sea temperatures above 10°C don't affect this score.

Paddleboard: hard fails are darkness, thunderstorms, wind over 15mph regardless of direction, offshore wind above 8mph specifically (a lighter offshore breeze still shows a caution note but isn't a hard fail), and waves over 1m (always too rough for a board). Wind and waves are weighted heavily here: every mph of wind costs 3 points off the score, and waves between 0.5–1m scale smoothly from no penalty up to a large one as they approach 1m. Softer deductions also apply for low tide, a sea temperature of 10°C or below, rain currently falling, and heavy rain in the last 48 hours (water-quality risk if you fall in). Sea temperatures above 10°C don't affect this score.

Beach volleyball: the only hard fails are thunderstorms and wind over 20mph. Wind is scored per mph rather than in one jump: every mph up to 15mph costs 2 points, and each additional mph from 15–20mph costs 6 points, so wind above 15mph quickly drags the score down well before the 20mph hard fail. Softer deductions apply for rain currently falling and very cold or very hot air. Wind direction, tide, waves, sea temperature and water cleanliness don't affect this score, and being outside daylight hours is noted but not penalised, since the courts are floodlit.

This is a simplified model for general guidance, not a substitute for checking official forecasts and safety advice — especially near any hard-fail threshold. This section is kept up to date whenever the scoring logic changes.

Wind, weather, waves, sea temperature and tide are modelled live for Kemptown, Brighton (300 Madeira Drive, BN2 1BX) via Open-Meteo. Water cleanliness is not live sewage-discharge data — it's estimated from recent rainfall as a proxy (heavy or prolonged rain increases overflow risk). Always check the Safer Seas & Rivers map before swimming. Tide, wind and wave data are forecasts/models, not tide-table measurements — cross-check with the Brighton tide table and Met Office for anything safety-critical. Sea temperature and tide come from a global ocean model rather than a nearshore buoy, so they can be a degree or two off what you'd feel right at the shoreline.
